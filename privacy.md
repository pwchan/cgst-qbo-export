# Privacy Policy: Migration Exporter

**Effective date:** 2026-10-2

## 1. Scope

This policy covers Migration Exporter, an internal tool used by China Graduate School of Theology, Inc. (CGST US) to access its own QuickBooks Online company. It does not access any other QuickBooks company, and it does not collect information from the public.

## 2. Information the app accesses

Data stored in CGST US's QuickBooks Online company, including customer records (names and contact details), sales transactions, products and services, classes, accounts, and custom fields. The app also stores the authorization tokens needed to connect to QuickBooks Online.

## 3. How we use it

Only for CGST US's internal accounting, reporting, record-keeping, and data management.

## 4. Storage and security

Data and tokens are stored on systems controlled by CGST US [e.g., "on company-managed computers and cloud storage"]. Access is limited to authorized personnel.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent this information. We share it only with parties who work on our behalf, such as our accountants or auditors, or when required by law.

## 6. Retention

We keep exported data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and then delete it.

## 7. Disconnecting

The app's access ends when it is disconnected from QuickBooks Online or its credentials are revoked.
